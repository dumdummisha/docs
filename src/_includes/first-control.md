Misha answers live in Slack as soon as its settings are saved, so know where the brake is before you need it. Once the settings exist, **Pause Misha** sits in the window's sidebar under your name, the Misha menu in the menu bar has it too, and `pause` works in your self-DM (your direct message to yourself in Slack).

Pausing stops new work at once: the clone answers only the commands you type in your self-DM, starts no workers and tries to stop the ones running. It keeps taking in what arrives in Slack, so those messages wait and are answered after you resume, however long that takes. It doesn't take back a message already posted or undo anything already done in GitHub, Jira or another service. To start again, use **Resume Misha** in the sidebar or the menu, **Resume** on Home, or `resume` in your self-DM.

[Pause Misha](/operate/manage#pause) has the full procedure, including what to do when a worker won't stop.
